## Design Context

### Users
Knowledge workers, researchers, and students who need to extract insights from complex PDF documents. They value clarity, readability, and a focused environment.

### Brand Personality
**Intellectual, Refined, and Human-Centric.**
The interface should feel like a high-quality digital workspace—more like a modern library or a clean notebook than a "robot" or a "dashboard."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Claude-inspired (Minimalist Editorial).**
- **Theme**: Light mode (off-white/paper background) with a high-quality dark mode alternative.
- **Typography**: A mix of a sophisticated Serif for readability (e.g., Source Serif) and a clean Sans-Serif for UI (e.g., Inter).
- **Color Palette**: Neutral grays, soft off-whites (#F9F9F8), and subtle peach/orange accents (#D97757).
- **Style**: Soft shadows, generous white space, and integrated components (no heavy borders).

### Design Principles
1. **Focus on Content**: The text and the document content are the stars. UI should fade into the background.
2. **Editorial Polish**: Use typography and spacing to create a sense of quality and trustworthiness.
3. **Intentional Minimalism**: Avoid unnecessary icons, borders, or flashy animations. Every element must earn its place.
4. **Soft Interactions**: Transitions and interactions should feel smooth and organic, not mechanical.
